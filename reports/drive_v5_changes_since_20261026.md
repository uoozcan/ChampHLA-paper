## What changed since v4 (26 October)

This page is not part of the manuscript. It summarises an editorial rewrite of the
manuscript against the results available on 26 October 2026: the approved release 3.5.0
development rerun and the registered FIMM and VenEx aggregates. No result changed, and
every number still comes from the same results registry.

### Results reorganised around claims

The Results now run in seven claim-led subsections:
1. The exact endpoint retained a selected subset of loci.
2. The vote matched the strongest single caller, but the comparisons were small.
3. No threshold or fitted rule improved on the vote.
4. Scoring ambiguous typing kept the conclusions, but rankings depended on which loci were
   scored.
5. Shared caller errors were mostly disagreements with the 2014 reference. This merges the
   error-dependence, oracle and re-typing analyses into one argument.
6. Call states, singletons, ties and vote support. This replaces the subsection titled
   "Unanimous calls were usually, but not always, correct", which held unrelated text.
7. Exploratory cohorts show that agreement is not accuracy.
