## Changes following the PDF comments

The manuscript now uses the title **Benchmarking consensus HLA class I genotyping from exome and RNA sequencing with ChampHLA** and follows Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion.

- The Methods explain subjects, loci, reference ambiguity and missing calls more directly, drawing on earlier drafts where their descriptions still apply.
- Four editable equations and an indented algorithm describe the implemented vote, tie handling, callability and concordance.
- Internal project wording has been replaced with descriptions of what was done. The main text no longer has a Protocol history section; the supplement explains the retrospective design and gives practical application guidance.
- Unconfirmed author metadata and declarations are kept in the separate author completion checklist.
- All figures and tables were reviewed. Workflow labels and crowded plot labels are clearer; wide tables use landscape pages, repeated headers and larger type.

The statistical results and their interpretation are unchanged. The development benchmark remains exploratory, and the extension and NCI-60 results are not added. Author declarations and controlled-cohort publication details still require confirmation before submission.
