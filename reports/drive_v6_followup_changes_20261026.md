## Follow-up wording edits after v6

This page is not part of the manuscript. This local draft revises thirteen
passages to make the prose clearer and reconcile the remaining inconsistencies
with the existing results and methods.

Main text: **4,008 → 3,990 words**. Registered result values and claim
boundaries are unchanged. No analysis or test was changed.

Long sentences now separate the finding from its explanation. For example:

- “Benchmarks show …, so the best caller … cannot be known in advance.”
  becomes a benchmark finding followed by “The best caller for a new dataset
  is therefore unknown.”
- “Of 2,642 source rows, 2,610 were imported, and …” now separates importing
  the rows from collapsing repeated records.
- The figure legend states the significance rule first, then explains why
  intervals that exclude zero can still be shown as nonsignificant.

The consistency corrections remove non-literal equality wording, retain the
limited panel scope, align historical provenance with the supplement, name
the recorded fitted caller, give the FIMM eligibility views equal status,
and identify subjects as the sign-flip test's unit.
